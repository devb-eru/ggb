# Third-Party Notices

No third-party runtime component or content asset has been approved for a GGB release as of 2026-07-31.

Godot Engine is the known planned runtime. It is distributed under the MIT license and requires its copyright notice and license statement when the engine binary is redistributed. The final notice will be generated from the exact export template and its third-party license inventory; the planning record is `GODOT_ENGINE` in the registry below.

The working registry is [`docs/third_party_credits.csv`](docs/third_party_credits.csv). Before any demo or full release, every external font, library, plugin, brush, texture, sound, music track, generated asset dependency, and store asset must be listed there with its license and required attribution. This file must then be regenerated or updated from the approved registry.

The absence of an entry here is not evidence that an unreviewed file is free of third-party rights.
