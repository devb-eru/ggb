## 인격 서명 6종

서명은 대사나 캐릭터 목소리가 아니다. 다음 기본 소리를 만든 뒤 각 상태를 추가 입력문 또는 승인 소리의 편집으로 변형한다. 관계 미완료는 삭제가 아니라 거리·잡음·응답 지연으로 처리한다.

| 부모 ID | 목표 | Styles 기본 입력문 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `AUD_SIG_EDGAR` | 0.9초 | `A low compact clock tone joined to a precise metal locking-pin contact and a very short straight bowed-metal stroke. Upright, controlled, responsible and dry. No threatening sword strike or military command.` |
| `AUD_SIG_MARA1` | 0.7초 | `A dry brush flick crossing a dull spanner contact in one quick diagonal sweep. Energetic practical care, tactile and concise. No cartoon boing, comedy sting or heavy hammer blow.` |
| `AUD_SIG_LUCA` | 1.2초 | `Two soft mechanical pulses, one lower and one higher, followed by a gentle response and a tiny ear-sensor contact. Tentative but functional life support. No monitor alarm, crying or realistic pounding heartbeat.` |
| `AUD_SIG_IRIS` | 1.4초+여운 0.8초 | `A thin glass contact opening into a short soft breath of air and a delicate radial noise texture. Protective and unreadable, not angelic worship or hidden-villain music. Keep the airy decay gentle.` |
| `AUD_SIG_MARA2` | 0.8초 | `A quick compact three-note nonverbal archive figure, a tiny frame-pin contact and a faint doubled contour. Clever, nimble and fragile without childish vocalization. No full melody or cartoon flourish.` |
| `AUD_SIG_SUBJECT` | 0.65초 | `A short graphite stroke pressed into paper fiber with a little fingertip friction. Personal authorship expressed only through dry tactile material. No electronic confirmation tone or magical shimmer.` |
